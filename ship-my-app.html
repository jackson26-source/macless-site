Before you ask

Do I have to give you my Apple ID or Google account password?

No — and if anyone asks you for it, that's worth being cautious about. Apple's App Store Connect and Google's Play Console both support inviting a collaborator with a limited role (App Manager on Apple's side, a scoped permission set on Google's) that can build, test, and submit without ever seeing your login. That's the only kind of access we ask for.

Can you just submit it under your own developer account so I don't need one?

No, and this isn't a policy we chose — it's Apple's App Store Review Guideline 4.2.6, which specifically bars submitting an app on a client's behalf under a developer's own account. If you don't already have an Apple Developer account ($99/year, paid directly to Apple) or a Google Play Console account (a one-time $25 registration fee, also paid directly to Google), we'll walk you through setting one up — it has to be in your name or your business's name either way.

What app builders do you work with?

Replit, Lovable, Base44, Bubble, FlutterFlow, and Capacitor-based projects are the most common, and plain native iOS/Android code works too. If your export doesn't produce a native build yet — this is the most common snag on Lovable and similar tools — wrapping it (Capacitor, Despia, Median, or a real Xcode project) is part of what we handle.

What if it gets rejected?

We read the actual rejection, using the same rejection-category knowledge behind the free Rejection Doctor tool, fix what it's actually asking for, and resubmit. This is normal — first submissions get rejected often, for reasons ranging from a missing privacy policy link to an ambiguous screenshot — and it's included in the price, not billed as extra rounds.

Is this the same pipeline as the self-serve Macless product?

Yes. This is the option mentioned at the bottom of the self-serve pricing page — the same build-sign-submit pipeline that ships Citolex, just with us running it on your project instead of you clicking through the wizard yourself. If you'd rather have full hands-on control and just want the tooling, that page is the one to look at instead.

How long does it take?

Depends entirely on how quickly Apple and Google review — that part is out of anyone's hands and typically runs 1–3 days per submission, sometimes longer. The work on our side (getting a signed build out and a listing ready) is usually the fast part. The 30-day guarantee window accounts for a slow review cycle or a rejection round or two.

What do you need from me to start?

A link to the app or the repo, what you built it with, your app's name and a short description, an app icon and a few screenshots (or the running app if you don't have screenshots yet), and — once we've confirmed scope — a collaborator invite to your Apple Developer and/or Google Play accounts at the limited role described above.
