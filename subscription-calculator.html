Worth noting if you're currently on Ionic Appflow: Appflow is shutting down December 31, 2027. If that's your current setup, the actual decision isn't "should I switch" — it's "what do I switch to," and this comparison is exactly that question.

Why the gap is this big

Macless isn't cheaper because it does less — it runs the same build-sign-archive-upload steps, on the same category of macOS runner. The difference is the business model: Codemagic, Bitrise, and Xcode Cloud sell you their infrastructure and charge for every minute you use it, indefinitely. Macless sells you the pipeline itself, once, running on GitHub's own free-for-public-repos macOS minutes — there's no usage meter because there's no ongoing service to meter. Part of that $99 still pays for keeping the pipeline working as Apple changes things on its end, that part just isn't metered per build the way the subscription services are, the changelog is the actual record of it happening.
