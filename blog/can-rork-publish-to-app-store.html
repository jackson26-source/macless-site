Can Rork publish your app to the App Store and Google Play?

Rork builds you a real app and then gives you a documentation link. The documentation link is where the actual work starts.

Jackson · October 2026

Documented and handled are not the same thing

Rork points you at an outside build and submission service. That means another account, generated credentials, a build configuration, and commands typed into a terminal, following instructions written for developers.

If you chose a tool that builds apps from a description because you aren't a developer, being handed developer documentation at the final step is a strange place to land. Rork isn't going to walk you through it.

We set that up, or fix it if you've half-done it already. You don't learn a second toolchain to launch your own product. Macless is an all in one, built, debugged and shipped. No handoff, no work.

A built file isn't a working app

The build service produces a file. It doesn't check that sign-in keeps people inside your app, or that push notifications run on real credentials instead of test ones that fail silently once you're live.

We check and fix both before launch, not after a customer complains.

The entire store operation is still yours

Developer accounts, listing text, screenshots, a privacy policy, the submission, Apple's rejection emails, the resubmission after. Rork does none of it.

We do all of it, under your own developer accounts, with your name as publisher.

Every change means another build and another wait

Fix something in Rork and the phone app doesn't move until somebody rebuilds and resubmits it, and Apple approves it again.

One step on your end with us.

The features you haven't thought to ask for

A notification that opens the exact screen it's about. A badge on your app icon. An Apple Wallet pass for a booking, ticket or membership. Face ID sign-in. A real camera scanner. The share sheet. Background location for drivers and field work.

Subscriptions too: Apple only allows digital subscriptions through its own payment system, and it's likely we can build that in depending on how your app works. Raise it before your pricing depends on it.

We can build the app itself, not just ship it

If Rork got you most of a product, we build the rest. The admin side staff use, separated from the customer side. A partner app and a dispatch app on the same data, each with its own login and listing. Data exports. Server-side logic.

Summary

Rork builds a real app and stops at the hardest part for a non-developer.

Everything after the documentation link is ours.
