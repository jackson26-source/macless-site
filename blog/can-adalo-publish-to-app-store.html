Can Adalo publish your app to the App Store and Google Play?

Adalo says yes, and it's one of the few here that builds genuinely native apps rather than a website in a wrapper. Its claim needs reading closely though.

Jackson · October 2026

What the claim covers, and what it costs

Adalo's own marketing says it produces real app files and handles compilation and submission, on a plan around $65 a month. That's Adalo describing Adalo, not an outside assessment.

What's true either way: the developer accounts are yours, the legal responsibility for the app is yours, and Apple's $99 a year plus Google's $25 sit on top of the $65 a month.

If Adalo's own process stalls, gets rejected, or hits a limit on your plan, we submit it directly rather than leaving you stuck on a subscription you're already paying for. Macless is an all in one, built, debugged and shipped.

A working app isn't automatically a correct one

Adalo building and submitting doesn't confirm that sign-in keeps people inside the app across every login method you've set up, or that push notifications run on real production credentials rather than test ones.

It also doesn't confirm that what your backend expects, specific security headers, correct return addresses after login, forms it validates, works the way the finished app actually sends it, rather than how the preview did.

We test that against your real backend and fix what doesn't match.

Apple's rejection is still yours to decode

Submission isn't approval. When Apple sends back something vague, somebody has to work out which of several causes it is, change it and resubmit.

We do that, and we write the listing, the screenshots and the privacy policy.

The features you haven't thought to ask for

A notification that opens the exact screen it's about. A badge on your app icon. An Apple Wallet pass for bookings, tickets or memberships. Face ID sign-in. A real camera scanner. The share sheet. Background location for drivers and field work.

Subscriptions as well, since Apple requires its own payment system for digital subscriptions. It's likely we can build that in depending on how your app is built.

We can build the app itself, not just ship it

Adalo has real limits on what it can build, and most people hit them eventually. When you do, we build past them: the staff admin side separated from the customer side, a partner app and a dispatch app on the same data with their own logins and listings, data exports, and server-side logic Adalo doesn't reach.

Summary

Adalo gets further on publishing than most of this category, by its own account.

The $65 a month doesn't cover what breaks in week six, and it doesn't cover the second and third app your business will want. That's ours.
