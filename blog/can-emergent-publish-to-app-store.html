Can Emergent publish your app to the App Store and Google Play?

Emergent describes building apps. It doesn't describe submitting them, maintaining them, or anything that happens after.

Jackson · October 2026

The gap is wider here because nobody's even pointing at it

Other platforms at least give you a documentation link. Emergent's own material stops at "builds cross-platform apps," with nothing about credentials, submission, store listings or what comes next.

So if you assumed the finish line included a live app in the App Store, that assumption came from nowhere. You'd find out late.

We fill the whole gap: the build setup, the server side, the real app features, the submission, the listing, and everything after. Macless is an all in one, built, debugged and shipped.

What nobody checked

Emergent's apps sit on the same foundation as several others here, which means two things need verifying and nothing in Emergent's process verifies them. That sign-in keeps people inside your app. That push notifications run on real credentials rather than test ones.

We check and fix both. That's diligence Emergent never promised, which is exactly why it goes undone.

The entire store operation

Apple and Google developer accounts, the credentials, the listing text and screenshots, the privacy policy, the submission, Apple's rejection email and the resubmission.

We run all of it under your accounts, with your name as publisher.

Every change means another submission

Nothing reaches a phone until somebody rebuilds and resubmits it, and Apple approves it again. That's every bug fix, forever.

One step on your end with us.

The features you haven't thought to ask for

Notifications that open the exact screen they're about. A badge on your icon. Apple Wallet passes. Face ID sign-in. A real camera scanner. The share sheet. Background location for drivers and field work.

Subscriptions as well. Apple requires its own payment system for digital subscriptions, and it's likely we can build that in depending on how your app is put together.

We can build the app itself, not just ship it

If Emergent got you most of a product, we build the rest: the staff admin side separated from the customer side, a partner app and dispatch app on one backend with their own logins and listings, data exports, and server-side logic.

Summary

If the assumption was that a builder carries you to a live, working, maintained app, that was wrong for every platform in this category, not just this one.

We're the part of the assumption that's actually true.
