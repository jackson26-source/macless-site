Can Bolt.new publish your app to the App Store and Google Play?

Bolt.new builds web apps in your browser. There's no App Store option in the product, and nothing it makes is an iPhone or Android app.

Jackson · October 2026

Same dead end as Lovable, with less direction

Getting a Bolt.new app onto a phone means taking the code out of Bolt entirely and starting a separate build process, with the same Mac requirement for the iPhone version and the same paid wrapper alternative. Bolt.new isn't going to tell you how. And none of that covers server side work or actual app features like notifications and wallet functionality. Macless is an all in one, built, debugged and shipped.

Bolt is very good at the first part of this and offers nothing for the second. We do the second part end to end. No Mac, no handoff, no work.

What breaks the moment it becomes an app

Three things happen to every web app when it gets wrapped, and Bolt.new's output is no exception.

It opens to a blank screen, because the app no longer knows where to find its own data once it isn't being served from a website address.

Sign-in sends people to Safari and often fails to bring them back, leaving them logged in and stuck outside your app. That can kill a whole app.

And push notifications don't exist, because a web app never needed them. Not broken. Absent.

We fix all three before anyone outside your team sees the app.

Apple's website-in-a-box rejection applies here too

Guideline 4.2 rejects apps that feel like a browser. The giveaways: a Safari-like loading bar, a login that forgets you, a web menu instead of bottom navigation, no notifications, no location, and the browser's offline error page.

That's a description of an unmodified wrapped Bolt app.

Passing means a real app shell: bottom navigation, a fixed header, sliding screens, a splash screen, a custom offline screen, in-app links. Push notifications alone won't do it, which is the first mistake most people make.

That shell is what we build.

Features your app doesn't have yet

Camera scanning, Face ID and Touch ID sign-in, offline use, background location: a wrapped web app has none of these. All four we add.

Subscriptions are the one to raise early. Apple only allows its own payment system for digital subscriptions, and it's likely we can wire that into your app depending on how it's built.

Every change is another rebuild

Bolt updates your web app instantly. The app on a phone needs rebuilding and resubmitting every time, and Apple needs to approve it again.

One step on your end with us. You tell us what changed.

And the rejection is yours to decode

Bolt has no part in your submission, so it has no part in your rejection. We take the diagnosis, the fix, the reply, the resubmission, the listing, the screenshots and the privacy policy.

The features you haven't thought to ask for

Notifications that open the right screen. A badge on the icon. Apple Wallet passes. Face ID. A real camera scanner. The share sheet. Background location for anything involving drivers or field work.

Normal for an app. Impossible for a web page. Work we do.

We can build the app itself, not just ship it

If Bolt got you most of a product, we build the rest: the admin side separated from the customer side, a second and third app on the same data with their own logins and listings, data exports, and server-side logic Bolt was never going to reach.

Summary

Bolt.new is fast at building a web app and silent on everything after.

Everything between "built in Bolt" and "live, working, taking payments and getting updates" is ours.
