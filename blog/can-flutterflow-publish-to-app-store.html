Can FlutterFlow publish your app to the App Store and Google Play?

Yes, properly. FlutterFlow is the one platform in this comparison that genuinely solved publishing, with real one-click deployment starting around $39 a month. No wrapper service, no outside build tool, no disclaimer.

Jackson · October 2026

So this isn't a post about publishing. Where FlutterFlow is solved, we won't pretend otherwise.

What one click still leaves you holding

You're the one clicking it, which means the Apple and Google developer accounts and fees are yours, the listing and screenshots are yours to write, and whatever Apple sends back is yours to decode.

Google also requires the first Android upload by hand on a brand new account, no matter how smooth FlutterFlow's deployment is. That one catches people on day one.

We write the listing, do that first upload, and handle Apple's rejection and resubmission. Macless is an all in one, built, debugged and shipped, so the leftovers around FlutterFlow's one click don't land on you.

The part FlutterFlow has nothing for

Your app is live. Then real users find bugs that never appeared in testing. Your backend has a problem FlutterFlow never touched. A customer wants a data export. You need a second app, a staff version or a partner version, on the same data, with admin accounts kept properly separate from customers.

None of that is publishing, which is why the platform that solved publishing has nothing to say about it.

The features you haven't thought to ask for

A notification that opens the exact screen it's about. A badge on your app icon. An Apple Wallet pass for bookings, tickets or memberships. Face ID sign-in. A real camera scanner for barcodes or documents. The share sheet. Background location for drivers and field work.

Subscriptions as well. Apple requires its own payment system for digital subscriptions, and it's likely we can build that in depending on how your app is put together.

We can build the app itself, not just ship it

This is the whole pitch for FlutterFlow customers. Stay on FlutterFlow for building and shipping. Come to us for the things it was never built to do.

The staff admin side separated from the customer side. A partner app and a dispatch app on the same data, each with its own login and listing. Data exports. Server-side logic. Post-launch fixes when real users find what testing didn't.

Summary

FlutterFlow solved shipping. Nobody solved what happens after it.

That's ours, and we do it well.
