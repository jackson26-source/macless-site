Can Bubble publish your app to the App Store and Google Play?

Bubble has over 4.6 million apps built on it and no built-in way to put a single one of them in an app store.

Jackson · October 2026

What it costs to get this done without Bubble's help

Getting a Bubble app into the stores means paying someone else to do it, and the established services are not cheap. Median.co, which wraps web apps into native shells, starts its small-business tier around $7,200. MobiLoud runs about $1,499 a month. AppMaker charges around $999 a month plus a percentage of your in-app sales.

Those are the prices for a problem Bubble hands entirely to you. And at that tier you're usually buying the wrapping alone. Not the server side, not real app features like notifications or wallet functionality, and nothing after launch. Macless is an all in one, built, debugged and shipped, and we're still here afterward.

The three things that break on the way

Your app opens to a blank screen, because it can't find its own data once it isn't served from a web address.

Sign-in sends people out to a browser and frequently doesn't bring them back. That alone can kill a whole app.

Push notifications don't exist until somebody builds them.

All three are part of the wrap for us, not extras.

Apple's rejection rule is the real risk

Guideline 4.2 exists to reject websites in a container, and the documented triggers read like a description of an unmodified wrapped Bubble app: browser-style loading bar, a login that forgets you, a web menu instead of real bottom navigation, no notifications, no location, the browser's own offline page.

Push notifications alone don't fix it. It needs a genuine app shell, and that's what we build.

The features that make it feel like a real app

Camera and scanning, Face ID and Touch ID, offline mode with syncing, background location. None of these come with a wrapped Bubble app. All of them we add.

Subscriptions deserve an early conversation, since Apple only permits its own payment system for digital subscriptions. It's likely we can wire that in depending on how your app is built.

Every change means rebuilding and resubmitting

Bubble updates your web app the moment you hit deploy. The phone app doesn't move until somebody rebuilds and resubmits it, and Apple approves it again.

One step on your end with us.

And the rejection email is yours

Bubble has no involvement in your submission and none in your rejection. We handle the diagnosis, the fix, the reply, the resubmission, the listing, the screenshots and the privacy policy.

The features you haven't thought to ask for

Notifications that open the exact screen they're about. A badge on your icon. Apple Wallet passes for bookings, memberships or tickets. Face ID sign-in. A real camera scanner. The phone's share sheet. Location that works while the app is closed.

We can build the app itself, not just ship it

Bubble gets a lot of products most of the way. We build the rest: the staff admin side separated from the customer side, a partner app and a dispatch app on the same data with their own logins and listings, data exports, and server-side work.

Summary

Bubble is a genuinely powerful way to build a web product and has never been an app publisher.

Everything between "it works in Bubble" and "it's live on both stores, working and maintained" is ours.
