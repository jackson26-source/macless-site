Can Lovable publish your app to the App Store and Google Play?

Lovable builds web apps. It can't build an iPhone or Android app at all, and its own documentation sends you somewhere else for that.

Jackson · October 2026

Lovable's answer is to point at other tools

Lovable's docs point you at a free tool called Capacitor, or at paid wrapper services. Capacitor is real and widely used. It also needs a Mac to build the iPhone version, because Apple's build software only runs on Macs. If you don't own one, the free option was never an option. And that doesn't include server side or actual app features like notifications and wallet functionality. Macless is an all in one, built, debugged and shipped.

The paid wrappers work, cost another subscription on top of Lovable, and still leave you doing the App Store submission yourself. Lovable isn't going to walk you through any of it.

We do the wrap and the submission directly. No Mac, no handoff, no work.

Your Lovable app is missing most of what makes an app an app

Documented as missing or unreliable in a wrapped Lovable app: real camera features like continuous scanning, anything running in the background, location while the app is closed, reliable push notifications, working offline, Face ID or Touch ID sign-in, Bluetooth, and smooth performance with large amounts of data, maps or video.

That isn't a list of nice-to-haves. For most products, that list is the app.

We build those in: camera and scanning, Face ID and Touch ID, offline mode that syncs when the phone reconnects, background location, and push notifications set up properly instead of half-working.

Apple rejects apps that feel like a website, and this is one

Apple's Guideline 4.2 rejects anything not meaningfully different from your website in a browser. The documented triggers: a Safari-style loading bar, a login that forgets you, a web menu instead of real bottom navigation, no notifications, no location, and the browser's own offline error page appearing inside your app.

A wrapped Lovable app with nothing added hits nearly every item on that list. Exactly what you'll get if you wrap it yourself.

Getting past it means a real app shell around your web app: bottom navigation with your main sections, a header that stays put, screens that slide instead of reload, a splash screen, your own offline screen, and outside links opening in-app. That's what we build.

Sign-in breaks in a way you can't catch in testing

Google sign-in, magic links and password resets all work by sending someone out to a web page and sending them back. In a wrapped app that trip back often fails, and the person is stranded in Safari after logging in successfully.

In a browser it works perfectly. On a real phone, from the App Store, it doesn't. That can kill a whole app.

We build the return properly so sign-in finishes inside your app.

Subscriptions are a problem to raise early

Apple only permits digital subscriptions through its own payment system. A wrapped web app can't use it without real native work, and Lovable has nothing for this.

It's likely we can wire real Apple subscriptions into your app depending on how it's built, so ask us before you build a business model that depends on it. Finding out after launch is the expensive version.

Every change means another manual trip

Fix something in Lovable and the web app updates instantly. The app on someone's phone doesn't. That needs rebuilding, resubmitting, and waiting on Apple, every time.

With us that's one step on your end. You tell us what changed, we handle the rest.

And Apple's rejection emails are yours

Lovable has no involvement in your App Store submission, so it has no involvement in your rejection either. You get a vague email, you work out what it means, you fix it and resubmit.

We take all of that: the diagnosis, the fix, the reply and the resubmission. Plus the listing, the screenshots and the privacy policy.

The features you haven't thought to ask for

A notification that opens the exact screen it's about. A badge on your app icon. A pass in Apple Wallet for a booking or membership or ticket. Face ID sign-in. A real camera scanner for barcodes or documents. The phone's share sheet. Location that keeps working while the app is closed.

A web app can do none of this. A real app does all of it, and it's work we do.

We can build the app itself, not just ship it

If Lovable got you most of a product and the rest is beyond it, we build the rest. The admin side staff use, separated properly from the customer side. A second and third app on the same data, a partner app, a dispatch app, a companion app, each with its own login and listing. Data exports. Server-side logic Lovable can't reach.

Summary

Lovable is excellent at getting a web product built fast, and openly has nothing for the rest.

Everything between "I built it in Lovable" and "it's live, working, taking payments and getting updates" is ours, and we do it well.
