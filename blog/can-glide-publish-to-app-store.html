Can Glide publish your app to the App Store and Google Play?

No, and Glide says it in its own help center. Publishing directly to the App Store or Google Play "is not supported," because Glide builds web apps that run in a browser rather than real phone apps.

Jackson · October 2026

Glide also warns you that doing it yourself is risky

Glide's own documentation says you can technically use outside tools to package the app and submit it, but that Glide doesn't endorse or support this, and anyone who tries does so "at their own risk," with no troubleshooting help from Glide.

That's an unusually honest warning and it's accurate. It also describes exactly the situation we exist for. We take a Glide app from "at your own risk" to live and working, including the server side and the real app features a browser app has never had. Macless is an all in one, built, debugged and shipped. No handoff, no work.

With Glide you're not fixing things, you're adding everything

Most platforms leave you repairing what broke. Glide leaves you building what was never there, because a browser app genuinely doesn't need any of it.

It won't know how to find its own data once it's running as an app. Sign-in pushes people out to a browser and often strands them there, which can kill a whole app on its own. There are no push notifications. No camera integration, no Face ID, no offline mode, no background location.

We build all of it: the data connection, sign-in that stays inside the app, real push notifications, camera and scanning, Face ID and Touch ID, offline mode that syncs when the phone reconnects, and background location if your app needs it.

Apple's rejection rule hits Glide apps hardest

Guideline 4.2 rejects apps not meaningfully different from a website in a browser. A Glide app is, by Glide's own description, a web app. Submitted without real native work around it, it's close to the textbook example of what that rule exists to reject.

What gets it through is a real app shell: bottom navigation with your sections, a header that stays put while content scrolls, screens that slide rather than reload, a splash screen, your own offline screen with a retry button, and outside links opening inside the app. Push notifications alone won't do it.

That's what we build around your Glide app.

Subscriptions, early

Apple only permits digital subscriptions through its own payment system, and a Glide app has no route to it. It's likely we can build that in depending on how your app and backend work, so raise it before your pricing depends on it.

Every change means another submission

Glide updates instantly on the web. The app on a phone needs rebuilding and resubmitting, every time, with Apple approving it again.

One step on your end with us.

And when Apple says no, Glide isn't coming

Glide has told you in writing it won't troubleshoot this. So the rejection, the vague email, the fix and the resubmission are all yours by default.

They're ours instead, along with the listing, the screenshots and the privacy policy.
