Can Thunkable publish your app to the App Store and Google Play?

Thunkable says it publishes to both stores "without a handoff to anyone else." That promises less than it sounds like.

Jackson · October 2026

What "no handoff" actually means

It almost certainly means you don't need a separate wrapping service, which is a real advantage over several platforms here.

It does not mean Apple's and Google's rules stop applying to you. The developer accounts are yours, Apple's annual fee and Google's one-time fee are yours, the legal responsibility for what ships is yours, and the agreement making that true can only be signed by you. True of every platform in this comparison, Thunkable included. No tool changes it.

If Thunkable's own process doesn't get you to a live app, we submit it directly. Macless is an all in one, built, debugged and shipped. No handoff, no work.

What the claim doesn't cover

Publishing successfully says nothing about whether sign-in keeps people inside your app across every login method, or whether push notifications run on real production credentials instead of test ones that quietly stop working.

It says nothing about whether your backend's requirements, security headers, login return addresses, validated forms, work in the finished app the way they did in Thunkable's preview.

We test and fix both.

Apple's rejection, the listing and the resubmission

All yours by default. All ours instead, if you'd rather not learn Apple's review process during launch week.

The features you haven't thought to ask for

A notification that opens the exact screen it's about. A badge on your app icon. An Apple Wallet pass for bookings, tickets or memberships. Face ID sign-in. A real camera scanner. The share sheet. Background location for drivers and field work.

Subscriptions as well, given Apple's payment rules. It's likely we can build that in depending on how your app works.

We can build the app itself, not just ship it

Thunkable has a ceiling, and businesses hit it. We build past it: the staff admin side separated from the customer side, a partner app and a dispatch app on the same data with their own logins and listings, data exports, and server-side logic.

Summary

Thunkable's claim stops at getting your app live. Ours doesn't.
